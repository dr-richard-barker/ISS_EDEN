Antarctic Space-Analog Testbed • Neumayer Station III

Biomass Production & Controlled Environment Crop Yields

During the 2018 Antarctic winter campaign, the EDEN ISS Future Exploration Greenhouse (FEG) evaluated fresh vegetable cultivation across 12.5 m² of growing area under a single compromise climate to demonstrate safe, automated food production for human missions to the Moon and Mars.
